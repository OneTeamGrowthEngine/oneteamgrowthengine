Pillar 05 · Culture · Release 06

The human system that outlasts any quarter.

Culture is not the posters on the wall. It is what your incentives actually reward and what your metrics actually watch. A commercial culture is healthy when the human system is measured, developed and self-correcting — as seriously as the pipeline. Most organizations measure revenue weekly and people never.

Culture is the pillar most prone to vagueness, so this instrument refuses to fake precision. It doesn't ship a data workbook pretending trust is a spreadsheet cell. It ships an honest assessment: the behaviours that reveal the real culture, the incentive mirror, and the small set of people-metrics worth reviewing like pipeline.

Culture across the three dimensions

People. Trust, ownership, authenticity, psychological safety — where bad news travels up fast and someone junior can disagree with someone senior. Level 5: purpose is the fuel, and the culture develops leaders faster than the org can absorb them.

Process. Whether values are embedded in how work actually runs — in comp and promotion, not on a poster. Level 5: the culture self-corrects, flagging incentive drift before it needs escalation.

Data. Whether the human system is measured as seriously as revenue — engagement, mobility, skill growth, burnout signals. Level 5: coaching investment provably moves win rates, and leadership attention follows the data.

The instrument

Every pillar ships a working instrument. Culture's is deliberately different — sharper questions, fewer numbers, because the intangible deserves honesty, not a fake dashboard.

The Culture Assessment

  1. The behaviours that reveal the real culture rather than the stated one — rated honestly.
  2. The hardest mirror — where your comp and promotion diverge from your stated values is your real culture.
  3. The small, honest set of people-metrics to review with the seriousness you give the forecast.
  4. Concrete, low-cost practices that build ownership and safety; culture is built by repetition, not offsites.
