For each of the 15 questions you'll pick the description that best matches your organization today. A simple rule keeps you honest:

  • If you can't show the artifact, it doesn't count. "We have a shared definition of "qualified"" only counts if it's written down and used.
  • Answer for the organization as it actually operates on a normal Tuesday — not on its best day.
  • Your answers stay in your browser. There's no account, no tracking, no send.
